\subsection*{Dynamic Programming}
Recursion:
\[\texttt{OPT}(v, i) = \min \begin{cases}
\texttt{OPT}(v, i - 1) \\
\min_{w \in N(v)} \left[\texttt{OPT}(w, i - 1) + d(w, v)\right]
\end{cases}\]
